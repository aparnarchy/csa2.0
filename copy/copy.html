CSA — App wording

This one page holds every word in the app. To change any wording:
  1. Click on any text box below and type your change (edited boxes turn green).
  2. Click Download updated file at the top — it saves a new copy.html to your Downloads.
  3. Replace the old file at csa2.0/copy/copy.html with the downloaded one (or ask Claude to do it).
  4. The new wording appears the next time the app is restarted or rebuilt.

Anything in curly braces like {name} or {date} is filled in by the app automatically — keep it, but feel free to move it around inside the sentence.

When you feel good, you do good. At work, happiness hinges on four pillars: growth, culture, compensation and meaningful work. The Culture Super App tracks how they change over time.

Meaningful Work — definition

Work that matches your aspirations and skills, is impactful, keeps things interesting, and feels just right: this is what I was meant to do.

Growth — definition

Scaling your skills and knowledge at an aggressive pace and expanding your scope, aligned to your aspirations, with solid guidance along the way.

Culture — definition

An environment that pushes you to be the best version of yourself, with people who inspire and support you. Freedom to experiment, fail, and strive together toward excellence, with genuine trust and belonging.

Compensation — definition

Fair pay and meaningful performance-based rewards, so high performance is recognized.
